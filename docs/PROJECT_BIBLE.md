# Project Bible

This document captures the foundational product context for MomPopCare during Sprint 0.

## Vision

- Help families feel confident and coordinated while caring for loved ones.

## Mission

- Reduce the cognitive load of caregiving through clear coordination and shared understanding.

## Founder Story

- To be defined through product discovery.

## Problem Statement

- Family caregiving is fragmented, stressful, and hard to coordinate.
- Important tasks, updates, and follow-ups can be missed when information is spread across people, messages, and memory.

## What We Are Building

- A family care coordination platform.
- A product centered on care visibility, shared context, and next-step clarity.

## What We Are Not Building

- Not an AI doctor.
- Not a diagnosis app.
- Not a generic health tracker.
- Not a document storage app.

## Product Philosophy

- Clarity over complexity.
- Confidence over noise.
- Coordination over isolated recordkeeping.

## Core Product Principle

- Families should know what has been done, what is pending, and what needs attention.

## User Personas

- To be defined and validated during product discovery.

## Jobs To Be Done

- Help caregivers stay aligned on care responsibilities and status.
- Reduce uncertainty about recent actions, pending tasks, and emerging needs.

## Care Journey

- To be mapped before application development begins.

## MVP

- To be defined after discovery, personas, and care journey alignment.

## Engineering Principles

- Build from clear domain understanding before implementation.
- Favor explicit product boundaries.
- Defer technology commitments until requirements are better defined.

## AI Principles

- AI may assist with organization, extraction, summarization, and explanation.
- AI must not diagnose, prescribe, or present itself as clinical authority.

## Security Principles

- Treat caregiving information as sensitive by default.
- Design for privacy, access control, and responsible data handling from the start.

## Open Questions

- Founder story and origin details.
- Primary user personas and role boundaries.
- Exact definition of the core care state model.
- MVP scope and sequencing.
